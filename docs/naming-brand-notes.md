# Naming and Brand Notes

Temporary project name: City Picks.

The product should work across Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Toronto, Austin, Denver, Nashville, and future cities. Avoid YYC, Calgary, or any city nickname in the master brand.

## Best 3 recommendations

### 1. The Move

Best consumer brand. It matches how people actually ask the question: "What's the move?" It supports nightlife, food, dates, friends, weekends, and spontaneous plans. It feels more like a recommendation engine than an events calendar.

Potential issue: domain/app-store ownership may be competitive. The brand may need a modifier for domains, e.g. getthemove, themoveapp, makethemove, thelocalmove.

### 2. Nearlist

Best product-descriptive name. It implies a short list near you, which maps directly to the core product: not every event, just the few that matter. It is city-flexible, app-like, and likely more ownable than common words.

Potential issue: slightly more functional than emotional, but strong for search and product clarity.

### 3. Good Picks

Best friendly recommendation name. It is easy to understand, flexible across cities and categories, and avoids sounding like an event calendar. It can support business placements without feeling too corporate.

Potential issue: a little plain, but that may help trust and memorability.

## Names to avoid as primary brand

- Anything YYC/Calgary-specific.
- Pure calendar names.
- Overly corporate city-intelligence names.
- Names that only mean nightlife if the app also needs daytime, family, outdoors, food, and weekend use cases.

## Placeholder decision

Use City Picks in code and docs until the final brand is chosen. Keep package names and namespaces easy to rename later.
